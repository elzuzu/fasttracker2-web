About

Fasttracker II by Fredrik “Mr. H” Huss and Magnus “Vogue” Högdahl (Triton, 1994–1999).

This is ft2-clone v2.24 by Olav “8bitbubsy” Sørensen, whose replayer is ported directly from the original FT2 source code. It is compiled to WebAssembly with only a handful of browser adaptations (single-threaded, frames paced by the display, files kept in browser storage).

Drop modules, instruments or samples anywhere on the page to load them. Saved files land in the browser's storage: use Files to download them. Browser shortcuts such as ⌘W/Ctrl+W can't be captured.
